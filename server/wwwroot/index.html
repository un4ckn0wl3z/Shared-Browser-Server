SHARED VAULT

Cookie and session profiles

Domains visited by the primary browser are added automatically. Sign-outs and cookie expiry on the primary browser are synchronized too.

Deleting a profile removes its cookies from every browser except the primary one, which keeps its own cookies. With automatic sharing on, the primary browser shares a domain again the next time its cookies there change. The vault is backed up before every bulk delete.
